00 / A NOTE ON WHAT'S CHANGING

For the past two years, the music player has pretty much been the heart of Can U Be. This whole website started because of one song, and somehow a small project I made around it turned into something I've kept working on for two years.

With V3, I think it's finally time for that to change.

The song that's been hosted here since the beginning was never mine to distribute. I made this site because I loved the song and thought it would be cool to build something around it, but at the end of the day, it wasn't mine to host.

I'm sorry for that.

So with the release of V3, Can U Be will no longer host the song.

I thought about just getting rid of the music player altogether, but honestly, that didn't feel right either. The player has been such a big part of this site that removing it would feel like removing a part of Can U Be itself.

Instead, I'm changing how it works.

In V3, you'll be able to choose your own audio file and play it through Can U Be. Nothing gets uploaded or stored anywhere. The music stays on your device; Can U Be is just the player.

V3 is also going to be a complete redesign of the site. I've learned a lot from building and rebuilding this thing over the years, and I want V3 to feel like the next version of Can U Be rather than just another update to the same site.

Honestly, it's a little bittersweet. This entire website exists because of that one song. I've spent countless hours changing things, breaking things, fixing them again, and adding stuff that probably didn't need to be added. What started as a random project somehow became something people actually used, and then somehow stuck around for two years.

That's still kind of crazy to me.

To everyone who visited the site, shared it with someone, came back to listen, or just thought this weird little website was cool: thank you. Seeing people actually use something I made has meant a lot to me.

I think this is the right way to move forward. Can U Be can keep the thing that made it special without continuing to host something that was never mine to distribute in the first place.

V3 isn't the end of Can U Be, and it isn't the end of the music player.

It's just the start of something different.

Thank you for the last two years. Seriously.

— matt
