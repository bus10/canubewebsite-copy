00 / A NOTE ON WHAT'S CHANGING

For the past two years, the music player has been the heart of Can U Be. What started as a small project centered around a single song eventually became the main purpose of this website and something I've put a lot of time and care into.

But after two years, I think it's time for Can U Be to change.

The song that has been hosted here since the beginning was never mine to distribute. While this website was created because I loved the song and wanted to build something around it, I recognize that hosting someone else's work without permission wasn't the right way to do that.

For that, I'm sorry.

With the release of Can U Be V3, the song will no longer be hosted by the website.

I don't want to get rid of the music player, though. It's been a part of Can U Be since the beginning, and removing it entirely wouldn't feel right. Instead, V3 is going to rethink what the player—and really, Can U Be itself—is supposed to be.

The music player will now let you choose your own audio and play it through Can U Be. Your music stays on your device and is never uploaded to or hosted by Can U Be.

Alongside that change, V3 is a complete redesign of the website. It's an opportunity for me to take everything I've learned from working on Can U Be over the last two years and build something new from it, without completely abandoning what made the site what it is.

In a way, I think this is a better version of what the site should have been from the beginning.

It's a little bittersweet to remove the song that this entire website was originally built around. I've spent countless hours working on Can U Be; changing things, breaking things, fixing them again, and watching this little project become something people actually used. What started as an experiment somehow stuck around for two years.

I'm incredibly grateful to everyone who visited, shared the site, came back to listen, or simply thought it was cool. Seeing people actually use something I made has meant a lot to me.

V3 isn't the end of Can U Be, and it isn't the end of the music player.

It's the beginning of something different.

Thank you for the last two years.

— matt
